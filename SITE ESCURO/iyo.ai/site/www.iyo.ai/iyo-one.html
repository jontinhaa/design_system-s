supernatural audio

Custom-fit audio for perfect sound

The iyO One is custom-fitted by a specialist, with soft bespoke eartips hand-crafted from platinum-cure silicone by artisans. This luxurious process delivers the most comfortable, most secure, and best-sounding audio device ever made.

superhuman hearing

8-mic array for hearing superpowers

The iyO One is the most sophisticated hearing device ever built, with 20 microphones and 8 DSP cores. 8 mics on each ear enable perceptual transparency and directional hearing, while 2 more create Active Noise Cancellation and blissful silence. Controlling volume is magical: simply rotate the device like a knob.

superintelligent agents

There'll be an agent for that

With AI, a new kind of smart app is possible — we call them agents, and you talk to them like people. Each agent does something different, like apps on your phone. iyO is building a developer community to create agents for every need. Use them with or without your phone — iyO One connects to the internet via Wi-Fi or optional LTE.

Real time translation

Babl

Hear anyone in the galaxy in your native tongue. Using advanced real-time large language models, Babl takes a directional audio input from the 16 beamforming microphones on IYO One, sends the target foreign voice to the cloud for a high-quality translation, and in real time streams the voice back to you in your preferred listening language.

Superhuman hearing

Owl

Using 16 external beamforming microphones on the iyo one, owl helps you to Focus on what matters by minimizing distracting noise in your periphery and augmenting voices and sounds in front of you. owl will help you hear better, especially in noisy places like restaurants or cocktail parties.

Ask anything

Sherlock

Using advanced voice search, sherlock helps you find real-time answers on anything — current events, weather, history, or random facts. Just ask, and it delivers, no phone needed.

AI music dj

Deej

Deej uses a natural language A.I. interface to control your Spotify account. Deej knows music genres and artists, and can help you find the right vibe with a quick conversation. Deej can play tracks, spin up playlists, and control your music — all just by talking.

Next-gen hardware for voice-first computing

iyO One combines two 4nm CPUs, 8 DSP cores, 20 built-in mics, and 6 acoustic drivers into a beautiful titanium and glass housing.

01

iyO One is designed to last all day depending on the specific use case. In Companion Mode, we are seeing over 16 hrs of battery life. Battery life will decrease when running Phone-Free mode, especially for LTE models. Our expectation is that, for normal use loads and mode switching, battery life will be over 10 hours.

02

The iyO One has 10 microphones on each ear, for a total of 20 microphones between the device pair. iyO has designed hearing enhancement features using beamforming technology that can enhance sound signals coming from directly in front of you by as much as 15 dB. We call our hearing enhancement audio app “Owl”, and it will come pre-installed with the first iyO devices.

The truth is, we all have trouble hearing sometimes, especially in loud environments. Though iyO One is not a medical device to help with conditions like hearing loss, auditory processing disorder, tinnitus management, or monaural hearing, we are committed to providing tools that enhance quality of life for everyone.
