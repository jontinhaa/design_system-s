Overview

iyO Wand

The iyO Wand is a revolutionary new kind of computer without a screen.  In addition to the natural language interface of the One and yO, the Wand can “see” your surroundings, unlocking a whole new capability for Agentic applications. Shipping in Q4 2026.

Superintelligent agents

With intelligence on tap, you can speak out loud with your favorite agents, and they can see what you see.

Superportable design

About the size of a fountain pen, the Wand feels great in the hand and easily fits in your pocket.

Superseamless experience

Magical interaction design makes using agents a delightful experience.

superintelligent agents

There'll be an agent for that

With AI, a new kind of smart app is possible — we call them agents, and you talk to them like people.  The iyO Wand allows these agents to see what you point to, unlocking a new level of capability.  Use them with or without your phone — iyO Wand connects to the internet via Wi-Fi or optional LTE.

superportable design

Designed to disappear into
daily life

The Wand is a shockingly compact computer.  At about the size of a fountain pen, the Wand feels great in the hand and easily fits in your pocket.  Carry your agents anywhere.

superseamless experience

Keep intelligence always on tap

A double tap of Wand’s action button will give you instant voice access to iyO’s expanding platform of agents and turn Wand’s Privacy Light green.  Holding and pressing the action button turns the Privacy Light red and allows the agents to see anything you point Wand at.
