Overview

VAD Pro

VAD stands for Virtual Auditory Display - iyO's breakthrough in audio spatialization with high precision. When paired with iyO's psychoacoustics software, VAD Pro enables artists to create and render deeply immersive audio on an entirely new creative canvas. VAD Pro is the first studio-reference quality spatial audio device.

Hi-fi ambisonic rendering

VAD Pro uses sophisticated, personalized psychoacoustics with built in head tracking to render an ambisonic dome around you.

A new creative canvas

The jump from stereo to VAD is bigger than the jump from mono to stereo.
Stereo will soon be obsolete.

Bespoke audio, made for you

VAD Pro is custom fit to your ear anatomy and auditory brain, providing extreme isolation and impeccable spatial rendering.

Stereo was built for flat sound. VAD opens up a full 3D soundstage, so you can place elements all around the listener — not just left or right.

From R&D to real-time

Quick start guide

01

VAD stands for Virtual Auditory Display - it's iyO's breakthrough in audio spatialization with high precision. When paired with VAD filters in VAD Software, VAD enables you to create and render truly personalized immersive audio.

02

What makes VAD unique from spatial audio is that it can render a continuous virtual auditory scene, rather than just single virtual loudspeaker locations outside your head.

03

Use VAD Pro and VAD Software to listen and create stereo and spatial music in any DAW including Logic, ProTools, Ableton, and Reaper. Spatial music creation depends on DAW. Please see your DAW’s manual for further information and compatibility details.

04

Because of its USB-C connector, VAD Pro is not compatible with audio bodypacks. If you would really like this feature, or if you have any questions about device compatibility or how to integrate VAD Pro and VAD Software into your workflow, please let us know at support@iyo.audio.
