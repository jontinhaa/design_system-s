Modern payments require modern rails

Fast, cost-effective settlement is necessary but not sufficient. Compliance, security, and productive use of capital must live together natively.

Built-in payment logic

Attach conditions directly to money. Release on delivery, split on receipt, block on mismatch. No middleware or counterparty risk.

Gasless stablecoin transfers

Move stablecoins without holding or managing gas tokens. Sui removes that overhead entirely.

Native DeFi connectivity

Connect settled capital directly into yield, liquidity, and financial primitives on Sui.

Instant settlement

Payments clear in under a second. No batch windows, correspondent bank delays, or reconciliation cycles.

Micropayments at scale

Enable agents to transact at any value because fees always remain a fraction of the transfer.

Institutional-grade infrastructure

Engineered for the compliance, control, performance, and scale enterprises require.

Use cases

Built for the next generation of payment flows

Support payment systems that need speed, control, and execution built into how money moves.

Cross-border payments

Remove fees and delays with direct settlement on Sui. Gasless transfer, finality in milliseconds, any day, any time zone.

Embedded finance

When payment logic lives in middleware, third parties must be trusted to enforce it. On Sui, rules are encoded directly into the asset, eliminating counterparty risk at the source.

Microtransactions

When fees exceed transaction value, micropayments don't work. Sui's gasless transfers make sub-cent payments genuinely viable for the first time.

Large scale payouts

Compose hundreds of payouts, revenue splits, and fee routing into a single atomic operation.

Institutional settlement

Replace multi-day SWIFT settlement with subsecond finality. Keep transfer amounts private but fully auditable for authorized parties.

Agentic commerce

Agents need to spend autonomously. On Sui, issue payment capabilities with spending caps and recipient rules enforced by the protocol, not middleware.
