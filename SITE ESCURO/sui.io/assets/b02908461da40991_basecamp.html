FAQ

What is Sui Basecamp?

Sui Basecamp is Sui’s flagship annual event, showcasing the latest product updates, developer solutions, technological advancements, and collaborations within the Sui network. It’s a two-day gathering of founders, builders, developers, VCs,  investors, and traders celebrating innovation and the global Sui community.

When and where is Sui Basecamp 2026?

Sui Basecamp 2026 takes place on October 7-8, 2026 at Marina Bay Sands in Singapore.

Why should I attend Sui Basecamp 2026?

Sui Basecamp is where the vision of the global Sui ecosystem comes to life. Product launches happen here before the rest of the world hears about them. Attendees will get hands-on and experience demos of what’s shipping next. The people building the agentic financial stack - protocols, wallets, infrastructure, institutions - are all in the same room for two days, sharing knowledge, connecting, and shaping the future. If you're building on Sui, investing in it, or trying to understand where it's going, this is the room to be in.

How do I register for Sui Basecamp 2026?

To register for Sui Basecamp 2026, visit our registration page and follow the instructions: https://luma.com/SuiBasecamp2026

How much does a pass to Sui Basecamp 2026 cost?

The full price of a standard pass is $299 USD. A discounted price of $229 is available until September 30, 2026.

What’s included with a standard pass for Sui Basecamp 2026?

A standard pass grants you access to both days of the event (October 7-8, 2026), including all presentations, programming, workshops, expo areas, and any official opening/evening events.

Are you offering travel grants for Sui Basecamp 2026?

Yes, a limited number of invite-only travel grants are available to help proven ecosystem builders, contributors, and supporters attend. Email [email protected] for more information.

Where can I find my event ticket?

Your Sui Basecamp 2026 confirmation and ticket will be sent to your registered email address as soon as you complete registration. If you can’t find it, contact [email protected] for assistance.

Are there press attendance passes?

Yes, we welcome media representatives! For press attendance passes, please contact [email protected] for more details.

How do I pick up my badge for the event?

Badge pick up will be available starting on Tuesday, October 6, 2026. Exact hours will be posted as we get closer to the event. Please bring your email confirmation and a government-issued ID to the event registration counter at Sui Basecamp to receive your badge. Note that you can only collect your own badge; you cannot pick up a badge for someone else.

Do I need a visa to travel to Singapore?

Singapore offers visa-free entry to citizens of most countries, including the US, UK, EU nations, Australia, Canada, and many others. We recommend checking with the Singapore Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) or your nearest Singapore embassy/consulate for the most current requirements based on your passport.

Do I need to apply for anything before arriving in Singapore?

All visitors must complete the SG Arrival Card (SGAC) with Electronic Health Declaration online before arrival. It's free, takes a few minutes, and must be submitted within 3 days of your arrival date. Apply at mom.gov.sg or via the MyICA mobile app.

How do I get to the venue?

By MRT: Take the Circle Line or Downtown Line to Bayfront Station (CE1/DT16). Exits C, D, and E connect to Basement 2M of Marina Bay Sands. Use Exit D or E specifically for the Sands Expo and Convention Centre. From there, follow signage down to Basement 2 where Halls D and E are located.

By Car / Taxi: Cars park at the South, Central, or North Car Park zone at Basement 3 or Basement 4. From the car park, take the lift or escalators up to Level 1 of the retail mall. On Level 1, walk toward the main entrance of the retail mall where the retail shop "Chanel" is. Turn right after Chanel and the Sands Expo and Convention Centre will be straight ahead. Once inside the convention centre, take the escalators or lifts down to Basement 2 for Halls D and E.

Once Inside the Convention Centre: Walk straight into the Convention Centre. Look out for Hall A on the left-hand side — after Hall A there will be a guest lift. Halls D and E are further along on Basement 2, the main exhibition level. There will be signage onsite that will direct you to Basecamp.
