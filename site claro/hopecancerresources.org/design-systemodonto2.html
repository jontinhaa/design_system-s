05 — Motion & Interaction

Motion & Interaction

Motion is restrained: one global .2s ease-in transition on every element, a handful of scroll-driven effects written in the theme's inline JS, and a 2-second image zoom. The stylesheet contains no @keyframes — there are no entrance animations to document.

Motion gallery

Sticky header morph

header class: header-unscrolled
HeaderScrollManager toggles .header-unscrolled ↔ .header-scrolled past 80px of scroll. The wordmark collapses to the “h” mark (.logo-holder-main.show) and the CTA group moves into the nav. Header transitions: all .1s ease-out.

Mega-menu

On li:hover / :focus-within the .tick rotates 90° → 180°, the item turns white and the submenu opens. Try it in the hero nav (≥960px). The positioning is recalculated by DesktopNavManager on load, resize and scroll.

Mobile menu reveal

clip-path: circle(0% → 150% at toggle)
MobileNavManager opens .mobile-nav with a .5s ease-out circular clip-path from the toggle button, traps focus and sets aria-expanded. Submenus slide with left .2s.

Intro splash

home · ≥780px · scrollY = 0
On load, a blue layer plus a white circular-mask overlay and the “h” mark wait 1s, then expand and fade over 500ms via requestAnimationFrame. Reload at the top of the page to see it.
Scroll revealsection.big_hero.animate-on-viewA white overlay with a radial mask (.reveal-overlay) opens as the section scrolls into view: radius 50px → √(w^1.8 + h^2.2).

06 — Icons

Icons

No icon font or library. Icons are inline SVG (.ico-arrow, .tick, hamburger), SVG files for service pictograms and close, and CSS content:url(data:image/svg+xml…) for social marks.

Social .icon.*

15px wide, filter:brightness(0) — always black, no color inheritance.
  • instagram
  • facebook
  • youtube
  • twitter
  • linkedin
20px in the footer: .site-footer .social-block ul li a .icon. The text label is hidden.

Arrow svg.ico-arrow

Two strokes in #edf1f4. In cards it is 24px with a 10px left margin; .text-color-dark flips the stroke to brand black.

Menu chevron svg.tick

White triangle. Rotated 90° by default and 180° on hover; fill turns brand blue on a white tab.

Hamburger & close

Toggle strokes are forced to white (blue when the header is scrolled) via .mobile-nav-toggle svg *.

Service pictograms

100 × 100 box with a 10px radius (.full_service_list .list .icon img). Full-color files; color cannot be inherited.
