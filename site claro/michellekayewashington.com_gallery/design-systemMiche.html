A fixed menu column, one shared point where every title starts, and a wall that runs sideways with the works standing on a common floor line. Spacing is fluid and measured, not stepped.

05

Motion & Interaction

Movement is slow to settle and never bounces: one ease-out curve for almost everything, a symmetric curve for the walk between pages, and scroll that stops when you stop. Reduced motion removes all of it.

2 curves · 14 behaviours

Easing

Ease · settle --ease: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) · hovers, colours, lifts, the room change
Ease in-out · walk --ease-io: cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1) · the page walk, the walk cue line
