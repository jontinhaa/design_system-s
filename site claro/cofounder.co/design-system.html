2 — Colors & Surfaces

Warm paper greys. Ink at five opacities.

5 — Motion & Interaction

Springy entrances, quiet hovers. Nothing moves without a reason.

Entrances use cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1) with 400ms staggers; hovers are 120–300ms opacity crossfades between stacked layers. Scroll-driven reveals on the live site are JavaScript (not bundled here), so only CSS motion is shown.

6 — Icons

Tiny, embossed glyphs. Pixel wordmark on top.

Icons are inline SVGs drawn at their exact use size (6–16px) with a 0.5px inner-shadow filter for the stamped look. Stroke icons inherit currentColor; filled ones carry their own tint.
