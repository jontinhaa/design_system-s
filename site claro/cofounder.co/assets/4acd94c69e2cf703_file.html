    Get started with Cofounder and run your business with agents.

    What is Cofounder?

    Cofounder is a workspace for building the company around your product with agents.

    You build the core product in your own codebase with the engineering tools you prefer. Cofounder helps with everything around it: strategy, design, your website, sales, marketing, support, operations, finance, and legal work.

    Work is organized into departments, and each department has agents with its own scope, tools, and skills. You hand work to the right agent, review consequential actions, and Cofounder keeps the work and company context organized in one place.

    Cofounder is not your core product builder. It can build your landing page or marketing website, but it is not intended to build or rebuild your main SaaS product, mobile app, customer application, database, authentication system, or product hosting stack. See What Cofounder Does for the full boundary.
