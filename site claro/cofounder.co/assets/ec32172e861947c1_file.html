    Agents are getting much better at isolated, short-horizon tasks: answering a search query, fixing a bug, writing a function, or calling a tool. But many real-world problems are not isolated tasks. They require steering a complex system over time: gathering noisy information, making tradeoffs, adapting to a changing environment, and coordinating many moving parts toward a distant goal. We call this capability Steering Intelligence. Today, we’re introducing CEO-Bench, a benchmark for measuring whether agents can play the long game. Blog: http://ceobench.com In CEO-Bench, we give an agent $1M in starting cash and ask it to operate a simulated startup for 500 days. The agent manages the company through a programmable interface with access to business databases, company management tools, and social media. The market is partially observable, noisy, evolving, and full of delayed and coupled consequences. This is not a simple tool-call benchmark. Agents need to write code, analyze business data, infer hidden market conditions, design pricing and growth strategies, manage product quality and operations, negotiate enterprise deals, and adapt as the world changes. Some findings from our first evaluation: Only Claude Fable 5, Claude Opus 4.8, and GPT-5.5 finish above the $1M starting balance on their best run. Only Claude Fable 5 finishes above $1M for more than one run. CEO-Bench also reveals distinct behavioral patterns across models. Stronger models explore and adapt across a broader strategy space, while weaker models often collapse into narrow or passive strategies. Top-performing agents write custom scripts to simulate the future, analyze company databases, and infer hidden information from indirect signals. We hope CEO-Bench helps push agent evaluation beyond short-horizon task execution and toward the broader question: Can agents steer complex decisions toward long-term goals? Joint work with Zhuang Liu and Karthik Narasimhan. Special thanks to Modal and The General Intelligence Company of New York for providing evaluation compute. Blog: http://ceobench.com Paper: https://lnkd.in/eNiE6g_z Code: https://lnkd.in/epQbrYyF #Agents #Benchmarking
