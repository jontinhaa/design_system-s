01 · Origin

Grown in the shade,
gathered in the mist.

For the last twenty-one days before harvest, our tea is hidden from the sun. Starved of light, the leaf floods itself with chlorophyll and L-theanine, the source of matcha's deep jade colour and its calm, even lift.

We take only the first flush: the youngest, softest leaves of spring, hand-gathered from terraced hills above Wazuka.

02 · Craft

Thirty grams an hour.
We don't rush stone.

Each leaf is de-veined, de-stemmed and slow-dried into tencha, then ground between two granite wheels turning barely faster than a clock's hand. Push the stone and you scorch the powder; we let it take its time.

What falls is impossibly fine: a powder you feel rather than see, vivid and weightless.

03 · The ritual

Four movements, ninety seconds.

A bowl is not
made fast.
That's the point.
一 · sift

Sift the powder

Press through a fine mesh so it dissolves without a single clump.

二 · pour

Pour at seventy

Water just off the boil, 70°C, poured down the side of the bowl.

三 · whisk

Whisk in a W

Quick wrist, never circular, until a fine jade froth rises and holds.

四 · drink

Drink it warm

Two hands around the bowl. No phone. Ninety seconds that are only yours.
