Three voices, one quiet.

Nothing hurries.

03 · The ritual

Four movements, ninety seconds.

A bowl is not
made fast.
That's the point.
一 · sift

Sift the powder

Press through a fine mesh so it dissolves without a single clump.

二 · pour

Pour at seventy

Water just off the boil, 70°C, poured down the side of the bowl.

三 · whisk

Whisk in a W

Quick wrist, never circular, until a fine jade froth rises and holds.

四 · drink

Drink it warm

Two hands around the bowl. No phone. Ninety seconds that are only yours.

End of the system

Take your ninety seconds.
