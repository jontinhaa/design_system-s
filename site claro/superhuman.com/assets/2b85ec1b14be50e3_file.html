AI detectors work by looking for patterns in text that are more likely to have been written by a machine than a human. The most common factors they look for include: Perplexity: Is the writing obviously predictable? Burstiness: How much variation is there in the sentence length and style? Style: Is the tone and writing style overly generic or repetitive?
