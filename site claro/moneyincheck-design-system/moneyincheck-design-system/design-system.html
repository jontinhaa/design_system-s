This window looks straight through to the Overview itself, which runs behind this whole view with only its fixed grid and #decor layer showing; the catalog scroll drives the page scroll, so the cloth bills, coordinates, coin and N–S compass travel as you read. The scrim lifts while this group is in view. Coordinates pop in brand green and settle to ink at .47; the bills fade in 1.45 s after the loader and follow the pointer with a ripple on the Overview. At 1080px and below, or with a coarse pointer, the page swaps the cloth for the static bill images with scroll parallax.
