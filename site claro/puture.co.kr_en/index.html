We check every step for ourselves, and finish one experience at a time.

01ConceptOne core.
02PrototypeAn idea that works.
03TestA finish that has been tested.
01Concept

One core.

First we decide the one thing people will remember.

> concept — audience, space, goal. many ideas → one core. out — what it must leave behind

02Prototype

An idea that works.

Not with words or pictures, but with a draft that actually runs. Decisions come sooner, and there is less trial and error.

> prototype — a draft that runs. try → adjust → try again. out — a direction that works

03Test

A finish that has been tested.

We try it on the devices and in the space it is built for, so problems turn up in the studio, not on site.

> test — the real space, the real devices. run → check → fix. out — ready for the day it opens
