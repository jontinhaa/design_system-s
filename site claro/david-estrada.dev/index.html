About David

I design sites in Figma and build them in WordPress, so you only ever deal with one person.

I’m David, a web designer and developer based in Mijas, in the south of Spain. I’ve worked with established companies to strengthen their digital presence, and with personal brands, agencies and startups that want a site with real character, one they can look after themselves once it’s live.

Most projects start in Figma and end in WordPress and Elementor. When a design asks for more than the builder can do, I code it as custom Elementor widgets, so it stays as easy to edit as the rest of the site. And if you already have a site, I’d rather keep what works and fix what doesn’t than rebuild it for the sake of it.
