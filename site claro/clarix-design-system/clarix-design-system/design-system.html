( 03 ) Foundations

Backgrounds

One live three.js scene is the background of the whole page and of this view: an ASCII shader field on #f6fafe (rounded cells tinted by the five-colour cycle, masked into drifting blobs, rippling around the cursor) under a transparent layer with the animated holographic model and a particle logo of about 34,780 points. The page's scroll drives it; each window below flies it to one scroll station of the Overview.

Hero · ASCII field and holographic model

live · scroll 0.00
rendererBg full-screen ShaderMaterial, 15px cell grid, radial + blob mask, cursor ripple · renderer (alpha) GLB model with its animation clip, MeshPhysicalMaterial #010103 + holographic onBeforeCompile shader (spectrum waves, purple fresnel rim, 8px ASCII cells square→round with warmth, radial reveal after the preloader) · canvases z 1 and 3, the hero title sits between them at z 2

Particle logo · assembled

live · scroll 0.83
window.logoParticles THREE.Points: logo.svg sampled at 450px wide (front, back and edge points) + 3,500 ambient points · spiral from a cylindrical scatter, pow(4) snap, bilinear blue/cyan → pink/red gradient × 1.4 · scroll 0.68 → 0.80 camera pushes to z 0.5 while the model fades out · tilts with the cursor

Finale · model fly-in and logo shatter

live · scroll 1.00
scroll 0.86 → 1.00 · model fades back to 0.95 and settles at (-0.4, -5.2, -1) facing the cursor · logo particles unwind to their scatter and drift, scaled 1.4 · the last viewport of scroll is a hold

Frosted curtain with gradient panel

.glass-container fixed full screen, rgba(255,255,255,.15), blur(30px), border-top rgba(255,255,255,.4), z 10 over the model · .glass-gradient-wrapper inset 4rem, gradient.png at opacity .5 · on the page scroll 0.40 → 0.54 slides it up (ease-in) while the panel grows 0.65 → 1 and rounds 0 → 24px; here the same curve follows this window into view
