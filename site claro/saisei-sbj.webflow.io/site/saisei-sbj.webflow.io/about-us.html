About Us

Since inception, Saisei has taken on the challenge of designing spaces that not only stand the test of time but evolve with it—enriching the lives of those who experience them.

Building with Purpose

EST – 2022
Our Origins

Saisei (再生) was founded with a singular vision: to redefine architecture by harmonizing cutting-edge innovation with the principles of sustainability and tradition. Rooted in Tokyo, our story is one of rebirth—a commitment to building spaces that honor the environment while pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in modern design.

Inspired by Japan’s blend of cultural heritage and forward-thinking technology, our founding architect sought to challenge conventional practices. Saisei creates living spaces that breathe, adapt, and regenerate—offering both functionality and a lasting positive impact on urban landscapes.
