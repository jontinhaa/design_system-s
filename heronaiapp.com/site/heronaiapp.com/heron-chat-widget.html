FAQs

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers

Does Heron change my model without asking?
Later
No. Heron always shows you the edit first and waits for your approval. Nothing lands in your model until you say yes, and every edit can be undone.
Which design tools does Heron Chat work in?
Later
Revit, Rhino, ArchiCAD and SketchUp, with more on the way. It keeps your context as you move between them.
Can it see my whole project or just the open view?
Later
Heron reads the model you're working in, so its answers and edits match your real geometry, not a generic example.
What if I don't like a change it made?
Later
Every edit can be undone. Since you approve each one first, surprises are rare, but you can always roll it back.
Do I need to learn special commands?
Later
No. Talk to Heron in normal, everyday language. It works out what you mean, and asks if it's not sure.
